MediaPipe's hand tracker finds 21 landmarks per hand, right in your browser. A geometric classifier reads the pose: open palm, fist, thumbs up, peace, pointing. No video ever leaves your device.

Known gestures

Honest limits. Gestures are read from landmark geometry, not a trained gesture model - they work best with one clear, upright hand facing the camera at arm's length. Rotated hands, gloves, low light and partially cropped fingers confuse it. All processing is local; webcam needs browser permission (HTTPS/localhost). Night photos, heavy shadows and very small hands can be missed entirely.
